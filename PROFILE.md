## Partnerships are a first-class route (recalibrated 3 Sep 2026)
At this stage of the company an opportunity we can only reach **with a partner** is as valuable as one we can bid alone. Entity eligibility is therefore **never a reason to reject** — it decides the *route*, and every fit carries one:
- `direct` — the Vietnam LTD can bid alone (open international tender, consulting, remote scope).
- `consortium` — we bid jointly with a local or specialist partner (e.g. a locally registered production company, a design studio, a freight forwarder), splitting scope and fee.
- `we-lead+local` — we are prime and bring a local partner for the licence, the local entity registration, the site labour or the language.
- `partner-leads` — a local prime bids and we take a scope (art production management, artist liaison, curatorial, design development, install supervision) for a cut.
- `subcontract` — the buyer is a large contractor / scenography firm / museum-fit-out company that will need an art-production or cultural-management sub. Includes tenders won by others where the winner will subcontract.
Restrictions that used to look disqualifying are now just route signals: Saudi CR / Etimad registration, Singapore GeBIZ registration, Japanese or Korean domestic-entity clauses, Qatar local-agent rules, "must have office in country" → `we-lead+local` or `partner-leads`. Construction / fit-out / MEP tenders for museums, pavilions, biennale venues → `subcontract` (watch or fit depending on how much art-production scope they carry), not reject. In `eligibility` name the *type* of partner needed (never a specific company unless the notice names it).

## Still reject
Security guarding, catering, cleaning, pure IT/AV supply, pure freight with no exhibition scope, vacancies, calls for papers, prizes and residencies for individual artists with no production budget, and news that only mentions a tender not open to anyone.

## Contract sizes
Anything from USD 10k consulting to multi-million production programmes is relevant; a large tender we can only join as a sub is still worth an email.

## Verdicts
- **fit** — core need matches §1–6 (or a partner could carry the rest), geography or publisher matches, deadline not passed (or unknown). Any route counts. Also fit: open calls for curators/collectives with production budgets in the geography.
- **watch** — relevant issuer or theme but the notice is a pre-announcement, a grant to individual artists only, an RFI, or the scope/deadline is unclear. Also: strong fit but outside geography; large construction/fit-out tenders where the art-production sub-scope is uncertain.
- **reject** — jobs/vacancies, academic calls for papers, student prizes, residencies for individual artists with no production scope, news articles that merely mention a tender that is not ours to bid, construction/MEP/security/catering/freight tenders, film-only production, anything already closed by more than 14 days, and the word "tender" used as an adjective.

Be strict on reject and generous on watch: a false quiet costs a contract; a watch costs one line.
