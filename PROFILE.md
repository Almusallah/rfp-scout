## Eligibility realities (note them; they are rarely disqualifying)
- Bidding entity is a **Vietnam LTD**; foreign tenders that require a locally registered entity (e.g. Saudi CR, Singapore GeBIZ registration) are still FIT if a partner or consortium route is plausible — flag it in `eligibility`.
- Licensed lines cover management/marketing/PR consulting, market research, project-management consulting (non-construction), design of website/logo/image, software/IT, office support. **Not** licensed: construction contracting, freight forwarding, customs brokerage, advertising. Tenders whose core is construction, MEP, security guarding, catering or freight are REJECT even if the client is a museum.
- Contract sizes: anything from USD 10k consulting to multi-million production programmes is relevant.

## Verdicts
- **fit** — core need matches §1–6, geography or publisher matches, deadline not passed (or unknown), and a Vietnam LTD could plausibly bid alone or in consortium. Also fit: open calls for curators/collectives with production budgets in the geography.
- **watch** — relevant issuer or theme but the notice is a pre-announcement, a grant to individual artists only, an RFI, or the scope/deadline is unclear. Also: strong fit but outside geography.
- **reject** — jobs/vacancies, academic calls for papers, student prizes, residencies for individual artists with no production scope, news articles that merely mention a tender that is not ours to bid, construction/MEP/security/catering/freight tenders, film-only production, anything already closed by more than 14 days, and the word "tender" used as an adjective.

Be strict on reject and generous on watch: a false quiet costs a contract; a watch costs one line.
